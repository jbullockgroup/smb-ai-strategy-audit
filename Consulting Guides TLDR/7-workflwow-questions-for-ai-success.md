# 7 Workflow Questions That Make or Break AI Success - AI Audit Guide Summary

**Don't rush AI tools**—audit workflows first like a doctor diagnoses before prescribing. AI amplifies systems: Efficient → faster; messy → messier. Goal: Automate **right** bottlenecks for massive impact (e.g., agency proposals: 2hrs→15min → more clients).

## The 7 Make-or-Break Questions
Ask per area (sales/marketing/support/ops/finance) to reveal goldmines:

1. **Where's the Bottleneck?** Slow step backs everything (e.g., slow proposals kill client wins).
2. **Where Does the System Break?** Forgotten tasks/customers lost (e.g., real estate: slow inquiries → lost buyers).
3. **Where Do Mistakes Happen Most?** Repetitive entry (e.g., accounting: invoice errors → wrong clients/payments).
4. **Where Is Time Wasted?** Hidden daily drains (e.g., consulting: 6hrs/wk reports → team-wide 100s hrs/yr saved).
5. **Where Could Automation Increase Profits?** Speed boosts revenue (e.g., retail AI support: faster answers ↓ abandoned carts).
6. **What Are Experts Doing All Day?** Free high-skill from admin (e.g., strategists on data entry → focus creativity/decisions).
7. **What Tasks Happen Every Day?** Prime targets (inquiries/scheduling/follow-ups/data entry/reports).

## Audit Exercise
- List business areas.
- Apply 7 questions.
- Prioritize: Daily/repetitive = quick wins.

**Examples**:
- **Proposals**: Auto-pull CRM data → templates + AI sections.
- **Leads**: Instant response + notify → conversion surge.
- **Invoices**: AI extract → direct to software (↓ errors/time).

**Golden Nugget**: AI frees people for value work (strategy/creativity)—not replacement. Succeed by simplifying **then** automating.

**Exercise**: Audit today—business moves faster when right processes automated.

# The 90-Day AI Playbook Every Small Business Needs - Summary

68% small biz use AI; 34% do more with less. **Low-risk 90-day pilot** proves value on **one bottleneck** (e.g., response time/conversion)—no big commitments. Measure real metrics: time saved, costs down, revenue up.

## 6 Steps to AI Success

**1. Choose One Problem**: Biggest bottleneck (e.g., slow customer responses → AI chatbot/emails; inconsistent marketing → auto-posts/newsletters).

**2. Activate Existing Tools**: No new buys—use embedded AI in Microsoft 365/Google Workspace/QuickBooks/Shopify (summaries/emails/cash flow/product descs). Reclaims hrs/wk free.

**3. Measure What Matters**: Track like an employee: Response time↓, admin hrs freed, marketing cost/lead↓.

**4. Protect Data**: 1-page policy: Approved data/tools, review outputs pre-publish. Stick to licensed software (enterprise security); avoid public bots for sensitive info.

**5. Train People (Not Just Prompts)**: 15-min weekly: "What saved time? AI mistakes? Keep manual?" Oversight makes AI reliable.

**6. Review & Scale (Day 90)**: Investor lens—ROI? Cust. experience↑? Yes → expand (chatbot to new channel, more tasks). No → lessons/pivot.

## Progression
- **Sprint 1**: Prove 1 area.
- **Next**: Scale incrementally (compounds savings/confidence).

**Golden Nuggets**:
- AI automates tasks/friction—won't replace relationships.
- Focus **one outcome**; humans oversee.
- Start now: 90 days → optimizing, not questioning.

**Outcome**: Disciplined trial → growth without overwhelm.

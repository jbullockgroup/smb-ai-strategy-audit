80% of AI projects fail due to solving wrong problems—successful 20% start with **diagnosis first**: Find expensive bottlenecks, quantify costs, prove ROI before tech. This 6-step framework turns audits into 100x+ returns for 7-8 figure businesses.

## 6-Step AI Audit Framework

**1. Define Clear Outcomes**: Vague "we need AI" → Measurable: "Cut lead response 3hrs→5min (+$161k/yr)" or "Save 12hrs/wk on tasks". Ask: "Most expensive problem?" Attach $ value always.

**2. Map Business Processes**: Interview 10+ trench workers (not just CEO): "Walk me through yesterday?" Visualize flows (Excel/Mermaid) to spot potholes/hand-offs/delays.

**3. Atomic Task Breakdown**: Drill "then what?" until unbreakable: e.g., "Follow-up" = 7 steps (open CRM→filter→ICP check→draft→send→reminder). Time/tools/decisions per step.

**4. AI Suitability Filter** (All 4 YES = Automatable):
- Structured input? (Forms/DBs=yes; vague=no)
- Predictable output? (Classify/extract=yes; creative=no)
- Rule-based decisions? (If-then=yes; judgment=no)
- Repeated often? (Daily/weekly=yes)

AI handles 80% routine; humans 20% edges.

**5. Impact Matrix Prioritization** (2x2 Grid):
|          | Low Difficulty | High Difficulty |
|----------|----------------|-----------------|
| **High Value** | Quick Wins (Start here: Prove ROI fast) | Big Swings (Phase 2-3) |
| **Low Value**  | Low Priority | Money Pits (Avoid) |

Ex: Property mgmt—Quick win: Tenant FAQ AI (30hrs/wk→3hrs, $42k saved, 3wks).

**6. Calculate ROI**: (Time waste/person × people × days × loaded $/hr) + Lost revenue.
- Ex: 8 SDRs ×2hrs/day manual leads = $166k/yr waste.
- Property firm total: $180k waste → $81k solution = 166% Yr1 ROI, 9mo payback.

## Real Results Example (Property Management)
- Lead response: 3hrs→18s (+$161k revenue)
- Tenant comms: -68% time ($42k saved)
- Maintenance: -58% ($12.5k saved)
**Total: $215k/yr impact.**

## Scaling Tips
- **Beginners**: 2-wk "Opportunity Assessment" ($5-10k, 10-50 employee firms): Discovery→Matrix→Top 3 recs + roadmap.
- **Small Biz**: Quick wins/immediate ROI.
- **Mid-Market**: Strategy/alignment/culture.
- **Diagnostic Q**: "If 500 new customers tomorrow, what breaks?"

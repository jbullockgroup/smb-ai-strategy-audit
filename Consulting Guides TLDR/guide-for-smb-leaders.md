# AI in Business: Guide for SMB Leaders

## Overview
AI is already in 75% of SMBs (83% in growing ones), but unmanaged use creates risks like data exposure and compliance issues. Leaders must shift from experimentation to structured governance for ROI. Employees save 5.6 hours/week on average with AI, but shadow AI (unsupervised tools) is common without policy.

## 4 Stages of AI Maturity
- **No AI Yet**: Competitors gain edge; risk of informal "shadow AI".
- **Free Tools Occasionally**: ChatGPT/Gemini for drafts/summaries; data leakage risk in public platforms.
- **Paid Subscriptions**: Better features but fragmented; only 47% have security controls.
- **Custom APIs/Workflows**: 53% ROI in law firms, 30% less stockouts in manufacturing; needs oversight.

## 7 Quick Assessment Actions
| Action | What to Check | Why It Matters |
|--------|---------------|----------------|
| 1. Survey Team | AI tool usage | Uncovers shadow AI. |
| 2. Review Subscriptions | ChatGPT/Copilot charges | Detects unapproved deployments. |
| 3. Audit Extensions | AI plugins | Data access risks. |
| 4. Data Policies | AI guidelines | Prevents self-defined rules. |
| 5. File-Sharing | Exports to external sites | Sensitive data exposure. |
| 6. Integrations | AI in CRM/workflows | Expands compliance risks. |
| 7. Ask Dept Heads | Time-saving opportunities | Reveals quiet experiments.

## Top AI Use Cases (Golden Nuggets)
- **Admin Time**: 76% report faster tasks; 8→2 hours on reports.
- **Leads/Content**: Draft campaigns, SEO, proposals (with human review).
- **Data Analysis**: Trends, bottlenecks, forecasts (needs clean data).
- **Communication**: Summarize emails, draft updates (preserve brand voice).
- **ROI Examples**: Accountants: 7.5 days faster statements; execs reclaim hours from inboxes.

## Risks & Watchouts
- Public tools store/train on your data (HIPAA/PCI issues).
- Hallucinations, over-reliance; AI informs, humans decide.
- No policy = exposure; IT downtime costs $250K+.

## Leadership Strategy
Align AI with objectives: Policies, managed platforms, quarterly reviews. Like cybersecurity evolution—from chaos to governance.

## Forward Path
Deploy secure, integrated AI: Centralized, private, scalable. Gains: Hours reclaimed, waste reduced, margins up. OSG offers responsible implementation.

## Actionable Insights
**Phase 1: Strategy (Weeks 1-4)**  
1. Map 50 top processes by volume/time/cost
2. Score by AI feasibility (data→complexity→ROI)
3. Pick 3 lighthouse use cases max
4. Build executive scorecard (weekly reviews)

**Phase 2: Foundation (Weeks 5-12)**  
5. Data audit - 80/20 rule (focus 20% data driving 80% value)
6. Tech stack rationalization (3 platforms max)
7. Governance lite - Decision rights matrix
8. Change champions network (not top-down)

**Phase 3: Prove & Scale (Months 4-12)**  
9. Pilot parallel to production (A/B testing)
10. Weekly ROI dashboard (time→$$ conversion)
11. Train-the-trainer model (scale learning)
12. Cross-pollinate wins across functions
13. Enterprise AI operating rhythm

## Terminology
Lighthouse Use Case - High-visibility, high-ROI pilot
Decision Rights Matrix - Who approves what AI spend/use
Shadow IT - Unauthorized AI creating risk silos
Pilot Purgatory - Stuck after proof-of-concept

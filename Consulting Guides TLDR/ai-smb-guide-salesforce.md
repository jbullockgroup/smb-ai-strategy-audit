# AI for Small Businesses Guide Summary

Salesforce's guide highlights how AI, especially generative AI, empowers small and medium businesses (SMBs) to enhance marketing, sales, customer service, and operations without replacing humans. It emphasizes starting with data trust, ethical practices, and unified customer data to unlock AI's potential for personalization and efficiency.

## Key Use Cases

**Marketing**: Automate content generation, campaign optimization, and first-party data analysis post-third-party cookie phase-out; enables personalized customer journeys and ROI insights.

**Sales**: Prioritize leads, summarize calls, generate tailored emails and sales plans; reduces admin time, bridging junior-senior rep gaps.

**Service**: Auto-generate replies, case summaries, knowledge articles; speeds responses using customer data for human-like, personalized support.

AI accelerates routine tasks, freeing teams for strategic work while meeting rising customer expectations for friction-free experiences.

## Building Trust and Strategy

Trust is foundational: 89% of customers want transparency on AI vs. human interactions; address security, bias, and ethics via guidelines, diverse teams, and tools. Prepare unified, clean data as "every AI project starts as a data project"; update policies for security like data masking.

Equip teams with free training (e.g., Salesforce Trailhead) on AI basics, prompt writing, and data literacy to boost adoption.

## Real-World Examples

**Wonolo**: AI chat replies cut handle times by 20% (5-10s vs. 510s), saving 23 agent-hours weekly.

**PaySauce**: Internal Einstein chatbot expands support expertise across industries using knowledge base; prioritizes data prep and internal testing first.

## CRM Integration and Next Steps

AI-powered CRM like Salesforce Einstein uses customer data for 360° views, predicting needs (65% expect personalized recall). Start small: Identify high-impact areas, unify data, experiment ethically, and scale with resources like Trailhead.

# 30-Day AI Pilot Playbook for SMB Owners - Summary (Free Template)

91% SMBs with AI see revenue boosts—you're losing 8-12hrs/wk to repetitive tasks. **30-day focused pilot** (not enterprise bloat) succeeds 67% via vendor help. Budget: $200-$2k/mo (1-2% revenue). MIT 2025: Narrow scope wins.

**Free 30-Day Checklist Template**:

## Week 1: Identify & Score (Days 1-7)
- Track time 2 days (6 categories: inquiries/scheduling/data entry/content/research/inventory).
- $ convert top 3 (e.g., 6hrs/wk inquiries @ $50/hr = $15.6k/yr).
- **Impact (1-10: time/revenue/cust sat)** + **Feasibility (1-10: repetitive/digital data/training)** matrix → Pick #1.
- Baseline: Time/cost/error metrics.
**Common Winners**: Cust service/scheduling/content/data entry.

## Week 2: Select & Setup (Days 8-14)
- 2hr research: 2-3 free trials; score ease/integration/training/pricing/trial.
- Activate top; test dummy data; train 1 power user.
- Metrics/budget set; soft-launch 20% tasks.

## Week 3: Test & Adjust (Days 15-21)
- Daily check-ins (time saved/errors/feedback/escalations).
- Day 18-19: Tweak prompts/triggers/handoffs.
- Stable 48hrs? → 50% scope.

## Week 4: Decide & Plan (Days 22-30)
- **ROI Calc**: (Hrs saved/wk × rate ×52) - (tool + setup) [ex: Chatbot $1.8k/yr saves $16.6k = 469% ROI].
- Quality: Cust sat/errors/staff adoption.
- **Matrix**: Green=Scale (2-3x budget); Yellow=Adjust; Red=Pivot.
- Plan: Expand/lessons/next pilot.

## Pitfalls & Fixes
- W1: No time? Use email/calendar data.
- W2: Paralysis? 2hr limit.
- W3: Micromanaging? Scheduled checks.
- W4: Early quit? Extend 2wks if positive.

**Beyond**: 3-4 pilots/yr → Maturity (Month1 mastery → Yr1 dept-wide).

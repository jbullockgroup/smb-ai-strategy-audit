# AI Governance for SMEs: 7-Step Framework Summary

SMEs close AI gap competitively but face outsized risks (trust/ops damage sans buffers). **Lightweight governance** fits small structures—no bureaucracy. Builds reliability/documentation for decisions; commercial edge via transparency.

## Proportionate Principles
- Map AI use/why.
- ID key risks.
- Embed oversight in existing roles.
- Practical docs.
**Goal**: Reliable control + improvement.

## 7-Step Framework

### Phase 1: AI Landscape
**1. ID AI Use**: Map tools/systems (CRM/marketing/recruiting/fraud/analytics—internal/3rd-party).
**2. Clarify Purpose/Impact**: Decisions involved? External effects? (High-impact = priority).
**3. Assign Responsibility**: Existing mgr/compliance lead; clear escalation.

### Phase 2: Risks
**4. Data Clarity**: Origins/quality/suitability? (Root of most issues).

### Phase 3: Action
**5. Output Usage**: How outputs trusted/applied? Maintain human judgment.
**6. Practical Risk Talk**: Impact-focused (not jargon); who acts?
**7. Ownership/Escalation**: Monitor authority/pause/record decisions.

## Benefits
- Avoid failures pre-escalation.
- Justify decisions.
- Meet rising expectations (public/regulators).

**Golden Nugget**: SMEs' simplicity = advantage—clear accountability without hoops. Continuous: Understand → Risk → Act.

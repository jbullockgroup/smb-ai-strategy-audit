# AI Readiness Score Framework

## AI Readiness Score Overview
The AI readiness score assesses SMBs' preparedness for AI adoption across five weighted dimensions, treating it as a strategic journey rather than a one-off project. Organizations scoring above 70% are three times more likely to succeed in AI implementation within 12 months, per Deloitte's 2025 index. High scores correlate with faster revenue growth (25% per McKinsey) and better outcomes like 2.8x AI results from mature data practices (Gartner).

## 5 Key Dimensions
- **Data Maturity (30%)**: Focuses on centralization, quality, accessibility, and governance; shift from spreadsheets to cloud databases like Azure for improvement.
- **Process Documentation (20%)**: Maps workflows to enable 40% faster AI rollout (World Economic Forum); document SOPs using tools like Lucidchart.
- **Team Capability (25%)**: Gauges digital literacy and change readiness, as 74% of SMB workers feel unprepared (PwC); use Coursera for targeted training and AI champions.
- **Infrastructure (15%)**: Evaluates cloud/API readiness and security; cloud cuts costs 20% (AWS), enabling scalable integration.
- **Budget Alignment (10%)**: Ensures ROI-focused funding; defined budgets yield 37% better adoption (Accenture).

## Calculation and Benchmarks
Score each dimension 0-100, then apply weights: Total = (Data×0.3) + (Processes×0.2) + (Team×0.25) + (Infra×0.15) + (Budget×0.1). Benchmarks: 0-40 (build foundations), 41-60 (emerging), 61-80 (strong), 81-100 (leader); most SMBs start at 35-55.

## Business Impact
Improves efficiency (15-20% manual task reduction in 3-6 months), decision-making, and long-term growth; phased pilots convert scores to revenue streams.

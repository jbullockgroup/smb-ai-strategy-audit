# SMB AI Value Playbook: From Experimentation to Execution - Cheat Sheet
*Nalo Seed, January 2026 - Synthesized from McKinsey, BCG, Stanford, Deloitte reports*

## Section 1: Core Framework
**AI Value Gap:** 88% of organizations use AI in at least one function, but only 5-8% capture significant business value. The advantage comes not from adopting AI tools, but from how deeply you integrate AI into actual workflows.

**BCG's 10-20-70 Rule:** AI success requires 70% focus on people and processes, 20% on technology, only 10% on algorithms. AI failures are organizational failures, not technical ones.

**Workflow Redesign Principle:** The question isn't "How can AI help this process?" It's "If I were building this process from scratch with AI available, what would it look like?" — McKinsey found this has the biggest effect on AI impact.

## Key Concepts & Frameworks
- **AI Value Gap** - The chasm between widespread AI adoption (88%) and actual value capture (5-8%)
- **Workflow Redesign** - Rebuilding processes from scratch around AI vs. bolting AI onto existing steps
- **Agentic AI Shift** - Moving from AI that gives information to AI that takes autonomous action (drafts, sends, follows up)
- **SMB Speed Advantage** - Small businesses can redesign workflows in an afternoon vs. enterprises needing 12-month transformation programs
- **Pilot-to-Scale Gap** - Most companies run AI pilots that never reach production; winners set clear KPIs and kill fast

**Using AI vs. Capturing Value:**
- Using: Subscribing to tools, occasional ChatGPT queries, pilots that never scale, same workflows with AI sprinkled on
- Capturing Value: Redesigning workflows from scratch, measuring with KPIs, training teams to work with AI, building AI into core operations

## Critical Statistics
SMB AI Adoption & Impact 2025-2026:
- 88% of organizations use AI in at least one function
- Only 5-8% capture significant business value
- By 2028, 29% of AI value will come from autonomous agents
- Top 5% of companies already deploying AI agents; bottom 60% almost none
- 93% of SMBs using AI to scale reported revenue growth (41% saw gains exceeding 10%)
- 82% of SMBs reported AI-related cost reductions
- 91% saw year-over-year ROI on AI investments

Common Blockers to Value Capture:
- Workflows never redesigned for AI
- No clear scaling priorities
- Talent gaps in implementation
- Automating broken processes

What Winners Do Differently:
- Redesign workflows first (not after)
- Set explicit top-down targets
- Invest 4x more in talent development
- Leaders model AI use themselves

## Actionable Insights
**Phase 1: Audit What You Have**
- List every AI tool you pay for
- Document actual impact for each
- Identify highest-value workflows

**Phase 2: Redesign One Workflow**
- Pick one high-value process
- Design from scratch with AI at the center
- Set clear success metrics (KPIs tied to business outcomes)
- Question every step: "If AI can handle this, does this step even exist?"

**Phase 3: Scale What Works**
- Apply the redesign playbook to next workflow
- Train your team on the new process
- Document and optimize continuously

**SMB Advantage Over Enterprise:**
- Redesign a workflow in an afternoon (vs. 12-month enterprise programs)
- Implement changes in weeks, not months
- Speed and flexibility are your edge — no legacy systems or approval chains

**Agentic AI Preparation:**
- Start identifying repetitive multi-step tasks agents could handle end-to-end
- Build clean data foundations now (agents need good data)
- Train team on human-AI collaboration before agents arrive

## Terminology
AI Value Gap - The disparity between widespread AI tool adoption and meaningful business value capture
Workflow Redesign - Rebuilding business processes from scratch with AI as a core component, not an add-on
Agentic AI - AI systems that take autonomous multi-step actions rather than just providing information
Pilot-to-Scale Gap - The common failure to move AI experiments into production workflows
BCG 10-20-70 Rule - Success formula: 70% people/process, 20% technology, 10% algorithms
SMB Speed Advantage - Small business ability to iterate and implement AI changes faster than enterprises
